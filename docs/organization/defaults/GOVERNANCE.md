# Governance

Each Lets Parley repository documents its maintainers, decision process, and
release policy in a repository-specific `GOVERNANCE.md`. When no local policy
exists, the organization maintainers triage contributions, seek rough consensus
in Discussions, and record accepted actionable work in Issues.

All changes require DCO-signed commits and the repository's CI checks. A
repository with one maintainer may operate temporarily with zero required
approvals to avoid deadlock. Once a second maintainer joins, protected changes
require one independent CODEOWNER approval. Authors do not approve their own
changes and owners do not bypass required checks.

This is a policy target, not evidence that external repository or organization
settings are enabled. Confirm those settings separately.
