An async standup is the same three questions as a live one, answered when each
person has a minute rather than in one room at one time. This page walks
through a week of running one. The reference for every rule is on the
[daily standup](/features/daily-standup/#async-standups) page.

## 1. Open one

Start a new standup in the space and choose **Async** as the mode. There is no
speaking order and no timer: everyone who opens the room gets the answer form.

For a standup that opens itself, a space owner sets a schedule — weekdays, a
local open time, a time zone and a window — through
`PUT /api/orgs/{org}/spaces/{slug}/standup-schedule`. Each scheduled day opens
a new async standup, and opening it ends the previous one. See
[Scheduled async standups](/features/daily-standup/#scheduled-async-standups).

## 2. Get a reminder, once

Each person can mint their own calendar feed with `POST /api/me/ics` and
subscribe to it from a calendar app. It carries one alarm per window, at the
offset they chose. Parley sends nothing else: there is no nag. See
[A personal calendar feed](/features/daily-standup/#a-personal-calendar-feed).

## 3. Answer

Write today, then blockers, then anything from yesterday. The form is
prefilled from what you said last time and saves as you type. It stays
editable until the standup ends, and an answer written after the published
cutoff is appended like any other.

If a blocker needs somebody, tick them under **Who do you need?** Only they see
that you asked, at the top of their digest.

## 4. Say when you are away

Under your update, **Away days** takes a first and a last day. On those days
you are listed under **Away** rather than **Not yet**, in every space you are
in, and you are left out of the team trend. Remove a range with its **Remove**
button. Only you can set yours; nobody can mark a teammate away.

## 5. Read the digest

The digest reads top to bottom: **Needs you**, **Blockers**, **Changed
commitments**, **Updates**, then **Answered**, **Not yet** and **Away**. Names
are listed without counts and without alarm colours.

Once the standup ends, only the updates remain. The not-yet and away lists,
and what the standup changed about commitments, are never stored and are no
longer served, so nobody's history of missed days can be rebuilt from old
rooms.

## 6. Look at the trend, as a team

On the space page, **Standup participation** shows, for each of the last 12
completed weeks, the share of eligible answers that were given. It is one
number per week for the whole team — never per person.

It is built so a small team cannot be read one person at a time:

- A standup day with fewer than four eligible people is left out, and a week
  with nothing left says **Not shown**.
- Weeks are fixed Monday-to-Sunday UTC buckets and the endpoint takes no
  parameters, so the window cannot be shifted to difference two answers.
- The running week is never shown, so the number cannot be watched changing
  day by day.

Eligible means a member of the space, not a signed-link guest, who is not away
that day. Treat the trend as a question for the team — is this format working
for us? — rather than as a measure of anybody.
