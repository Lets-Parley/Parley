A retrospective in Parley is not a core session kind. It is a plugin: the
package under `plugins/retrospective/` declares the kind, the Wasm guest stores
the board, and the iframe UI fills the room. Enabling the install is what puts
**Retrospective** on the create dialog; there is no `KindDef` for it in the
shipped frontend.

## What the room does

- Three columns to start: went well, to improve, puzzles.
- Anyone can add a card. The author is stored, and **hidden from the wire
  state until the facilitator reveals**.
- Cards in the same column can be grouped.
- Each person may put one dot on a card (a second click does not stack).
- Action items are a list on the same board. An action with no owner typed
  reads **Unassigned**; anyone can name an owner later, or delete the action,
  from its menu.
- **A note's words can be edited** by whoever wrote it, and by nobody else,
  the facilitator included. "Edit note" in its menu, `E` or `F2` with focus
  on the card's words, or a double click on them edits them in place: `Enter` saves,
  `Shift`+`Enter` is a new line, `Escape` cancels. The card keeps its place,
  group, votes, stickers and links, and says "edited" from then on, since votes
  cast on the old words now sit on the new ones. Who edited is never shown.
  Before the reveal the board does not know whose a card is, so Edit is offered
  to everyone and the server answers.
- **A note can be deleted** by whoever wrote it, or by the facilitator, from
  the note's menu, after a confirmation. Its votes, stickers and links go with
  it. Before the reveal the board does not know whose a note is, so Delete is
  offered to everyone and the server answers; the removal is announced as "A
  note was removed", never as whose. Anonymity before the reveal means that
  nobody else can see who wrote a note; the server will still tell you a note
  is not yours when you try to delete it.
- Revealing authors asks for a second confirmation. Once revealed, each card
  shows its author's name. The facilitator can **hide authors again** in one
  press: the wire state stops carrying them, though anyone who was looking has
  already seen them. Only the facilitator is offered either control; everyone
  else is told who holds it.
- Selecting cards opens a bar that says how many are selected and why they can
  or cannot be grouped yet. A group is named when it is made.
- A card you add waits in its lane, drawn dashed, until the server has it. If
  the server refuses, the reason is shown and the text is kept.
- The facilitator moves the room through four **stages**: Write, Group, Vote,
  Decide, forward or back. A stage changes what the board puts in front of
  people (the composer in Write, checkboxes in Group, the thumbs kept in sight in Vote,
  the action target in Decide) and **blocks nothing**: a late card, vote or
  action is still accepted in any stage.
- Each control on a card does one thing. The **handle** in front of its text
  drags it and opens nothing. The **three dots** after its text open its
  **menu**, in every stage and always in the same place: Edit, Add a sticker,
  Start an action, Select to group, a **Reorder** strip of four buttons, **Move
  to…** (one step in: the other columns and groups) and Delete on its own at
  the foot. That makes the menu the keyboard and touch path for every pointer
  gesture; voting is on the card itself. The menu never runs off the board: it
  opens above its button when there is no room below, and on a phone it is a
  sheet along the bottom edge with a Done button. A group has the same pair in
  its heading.
- **Order is shared.** Drag a card by its handle, use the Reorder buttons
  (to top, up, down, to bottom) or "Move to…" in its menu, or press
  `Alt`+`↑`/`↓` (`Shift` for top and bottom) and `Alt`+`←`/`→` for the next
  column. A group moves as one, by its own handle or from its own menu, also
  to another column.
- **A drop goes where it is aimed.** Dragged over another column, that column
  is outlined and the card lands where the dashed slot shows, or at the end
  when dropped on empty space. Dragged over a group, the group is outlined and
  the card joins it at that place, also from another column. Held for a moment
  over the middle of a loose card in the same column, that card is marked
  "Group with this": dropping there asks for the group's name and makes a
  group of the two, and Cancel leaves both where they were. A grouped card
  dropped among loose cards or in another column leaves its group; a group
  keeps going with one card. Every drop sends the same request as the matching
  menu item ("Move to…" a column or a group, or Select to group and the Group
  bar), `Escape` puts the card back, and holding it at the top or bottom edge
  scrolls the page.
- **Voting is a thumb up and a thumb down** on every card, in every stage: one
  vote per person per card. The two thumbs are small buttons on the card's
  lower edge; point at the card (or Tab to them) to see them, and they are
  always in sight in the Vote stage and on a touch screen. Press a thumb to
  vote; press the other to switch; press your own again to take it back; or
  press `U` or `D` with focus on the card's words. Nobody can see who voted
  which way. After a reload the board no longer knows which thumb is yours, so
  the first press sets it again (which changes nothing) rather than removing
  it.
- **A card's score is its ups less its downs**, written with its sign on a
  small tag on the card's top corner: six up and one down is **+5**, a tie is
  **±0**, and a card nobody has voted on has no tag. Point at the card to see
  the split ("6 up · 1 down"). A group's heading gives the group's score the
  same way. If a vote does not go through, the board tries once more by
  itself and then says so on the card, with a **Try again** button; the score
  does not change until a vote lands.
- **Top rated** re-sorts a column for one reader only, by ups less downs, and
  writes nothing.
  While it is on, cards in that column cannot be given a new place, and it says
  so; a card can still be carried out of it, into one of its groups, or into
  it from another column, where it is added at the end of the shared order.
  The facilitator can apply the vote order for everyone.
- **Stickers.** Anyone can put a sticker on a card. There are fourteen: seven
  meanings (Me too, Thank you, Great idea, Quick win, Needs a chat, Blocker,
  Made me laugh), each as a vinyl sticker and as pixel art, and the two sets
  mix freely. Hover a card and press the dashed **+**, press `S` with focus on
  the card's words, or use "Add a sticker" in its menu: the sticker book opens with the
  two sheets, `1` to `7` place from the marked sheet and `V` and `P` change
  sheets. A sticker picked from the book lands on no word, in room every card
  already has around it, so nothing moves when one arrives; only a sticker
  somebody drags can cover words. Pixel stickers are never tilted, stay
  crisp at any zoom, and kick up a little pixel dust when you set one down;
  vinyl ones keep a small tilt.
- **Stickers go anywhere and pile up.** Whoever placed a sticker can drag it
  anywhere on its card, a little over the edges included, and so can the
  facilitator. Stickers overlap freely, the newest on top, and one that is
  moved goes on top; "Bring to front" (`F`) does that without moving it. With a
  sticker focused, the arrow keys move it (`Shift` for bigger steps), `Page Up`
  and `Page Down` go through the pile, and `Delete` removes it; a card's pile
  is one Tab stop, and "Stickers (n)…" in the card's menu reaches every
  one without aiming. A card's controls always stay on top of its stickers,
  and stickers lying over a card's words go faint while you point at the words
  (or tap them), so covered text can still be read.
- **Nobody can see who placed a sticker**: the server records it only to
  enforce that rule and the limits below, and never publishes it. So only a
  sticker you placed in this visit looks movable; after a reload you can still
  remove one of yours from its menu, and the server refuses anyone else's.
- **An action can come from a card.** The target on a card (or "Start an
  action…" in its menu) opens a small form; the action then
  shows the card it came from, and the card shows that it has an action. More
  cards can be tied to an existing action, and untied.
- **A timer** the facilitator can start (1, 3, 5, 10 minutes or a custom
  1–180), pause, extend by a minute, and clear. It is counted on the server's
  clock and published as time remaining. It never blocks anything: when it
  runs out the board says "Time's up" and keeps working. Changing the stage
  clears it.
- Typing is never interrupted: other people's changes update the board in
  place and are announced to screen readers.

CSV export is the host's: it flattens the guest's `on_session_state` document
(`revealed`, `stage`, `timer`, `stamps`, `columns`, `cards`, `groups`,
`actionItems`) next to `id,kind,title,phase`. Authorship that has not been
revealed is not in that document, so it is not in the file; who voted and who
placed a sticker are never in it.

## Actions and limits

| Action | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `add-card`, `group-cards`, `vote`, `add-action` | anyone in the room | as before; `add-action` also takes `sourceIds`, and a blank `owner` means nobody |
| `delete-card` | the card's author | delete a card with its votes, stickers and links |
| `edit-card` | the card's author | change a card's words; everything else on it stays |
| `vote` | anyone | `value` is `up`, `down` or `none`; it sets the caller's vote and never toggles it |
| `set-owner`, `delete-action` | anyone | name or clear an action's owner; delete an action |
| `move-card`, `move-group` | anyone | reorder a card or a group, move a card to another column or group, move a group to another column |
| `stamp`, `move-stamp`, `remove-stamp` | anyone; move and remove only for whoever placed it | place, move (which also puts it on top), remove a sticker; the actions keep their first name |
| `link-action` | anyone | tie a card or group to an action, or untie it |
| `reveal`, `conceal` | facilitator | show or hide authors |
| `set-stage` | facilitator | set the stage, 0 to 3 |
| `timer` | facilitator | `start`, `pause`, `resume`, `add`, `clear` |
| `order-by-votes` | facilitator | reorder one column by ups less downs for everyone |
| `moderate-stamp` | facilitator | move or remove any sticker |
| `moderate-card` | facilitator | delete any card |

Facilitator-only actions are refused by the host before the plugin runs.
Everything else is validated by the guest: unknown ids, text that is empty or
too long, coordinates outside the card, somebody else's card or sticker, and
anything over a limit are **declined** and change nothing. The guest answers
the host with one of four codes (`invalid`, `forbidden`, `not-found`,
`conflict`) instead of failing, so a participant sending nonsense cannot count
against the plugin; that needs a host that understands the answer, and an
older host treats a declined request as accepted with no change.

- A board holds at most **120 cards**, **30 from any one person**, each up to
  **500 characters**; **40 groups**, named in up to 80 characters; **1,000
  votes**; **30 action items** of up to 500 characters, with an owner of up to
  64.
- A card holds at most **12 stickers**, **3 from any one person**; a board holds
  **300**, **60 from any one person**.
- A timer runs from **10 seconds to 3 hours**.
- An action links to at most **12** cards or groups.
- A group is removed when its last card leaves it.
- An action's owner is a typed name; a bare user id is declined.

Every board of an org shares the plugin's one storage quota (1 MiB). A board
at every limit at once, written in characters that take three bytes each, is
about 340 KB; an ordinary one is a few tens of kilobytes. When that store is
full, a change that would grow a board is declined and the board says so;
deleting cards or action items still works, and frees room.

The new actions do not change what the plugin is granted. An upgrade from
0.1.0 asks for the same three grants, so it applies without a new consent.

## Installing it

Take the retrospective's `.parley` bundle (a release asset, signed when the
release key is configured, otherwise named `-UNSIGNED.parley`), trust its signer,
upload it on the plugin catalog page, and install it for an org; see
[Plugins: operate](/operations/plugins/). The grants it asks for are `kv` (scope
`board`), `session:read`, and `session:act`. Consent copy for those comes from
the host, not from this page.

Disable mid-session and the room shows the existing "kind unavailable" state;
enable it again and the kind returns. Uninstall is refused while any
retrospective session still exists. Those two rules are host behavior.

## One document per session

The plugin key-value store can get and set a key. It cannot list keys or scan a
prefix. Grouping and vote tallies therefore live on **one namespaced document**
per room. Writes are read-modify-write and last-write-wins; compare-and-swap is
not on this package.

The UI never fetches. `connect-src 'none'` is the frame's policy; every byte it
sees arrives over the MessageChannel bridge, including design tokens.
